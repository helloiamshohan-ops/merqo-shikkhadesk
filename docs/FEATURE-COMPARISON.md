## v1.0 scope decision [ANALYSIS]

**v1.0 = Tier A (core coaching operations) + full Tier B (complete double-entry
accounting).** This is the "agreed full core product and complete accounting."
Tier C (multi-branch, question banks, ID cards/certificates, library, inventory,
portal) is **staged to later releases**, and that omission is documented here and
in `FEATURE-MATRIX.md` for owner approval (DEC-5).

Optional integrations (SMS, gateways, biometric, portal) are **not** part of v1.0
and must not be advertised as available.
