# PRODUCT POSITIONING — MERQO ShikkhaDesk

**Research date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** strategic positioning [ANALYSIS],
grounded in `COMPETITOR-RESEARCH.md` facts.

---

## One-line positioning

> **MERQO ShikkhaDesk** is the **offline-first Windows desktop** software that
> runs a Bangladesh coaching center's academics **and** its full double-entry
> accounts — bought once (perpetual-use license), owned by the institution, with
> its data staying on the institution's own computers.

---

## The problem we solve [ANALYSIS]

Coaching-center owners in Bangladesh overwhelmingly run on paper registers and
spreadsheets. The software that exists is almost entirely **cloud SaaS with
recurring fees** where the **vendor holds the data**. That creates three real
frictions for a coaching-center owner:

1. **Connectivity & cost.** Work stops or degrades without internet; recurring
   per-student/monthly fees add up and never end.
2. **Data ownership.** The institution's student and financial records live on a
   vendor's servers under the vendor's terms.
3. **Shallow accounting.** "Accounts" usually means an income/expense list and a
   single balance sheet — not a balanced double-entry system an accountant can
   trust.

---

## Target customer

- **Primary:** small-to-mid coaching centers (one site, a handful of staff
  computers) that want to stop using paper and own their data.
- **Secondary:** larger, professionally-managed coaching institutions that need
  real accounting, roles/permissions, audit history, and LAN operation across up
  to five computers.

**Explicitly not targeting (v1):** institutions that require cloud access from
anywhere, parent/student mobile portals, or live SMS/gateway/biometric
integrations as a condition of use. Those remain optional add-ons.

## Differentiation (the wedge) [ANALYSIS]

| Axis | Market default | ShikkhaDesk |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Deployment | Cloud SaaS | **Offline-first Windows desktop + LAN** |
| Cost model | Recurring per-student/monthly | **Perpetual-use license** |
| Data | On vendor servers | **On the institution's own computers** |
| Accounting | Income/expense + one balance sheet | **Full double-entry engine** |
| Activation | Online account/login | **Signed, institution-bound, offline activation** |
| Device control | Vendor-managed | **Institution device registration (≤5)** |
| Language | Mixed / web | **Simple Bangla, desktop-native** |

We do **not** claim to be "the best in the market." We build measurable features,
test them, and document the evidence (per the product principles).

---

## Value proposition by persona

- **Owner:** "One purchase, works offline, my data stays mine, and the accounts
  actually balance."
- **Accountant:** "Real journals, ledger, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet,
  cashbook/bankbook — the numbers reconcile."
- **Teacher/office staff:** "Simple Bangla screens for admission, attendance,
  marks and fee collection — little training needed."

---

## What we deliberately do NOT do in v1

To protect the offline/ownership/perpetual positioning and ship reliably, v1 does
**not** depend on: cloud subscriptions, mandatory online login/activation refresh,
live SMS/WhatsApp, payment-gateway APIs, or biometric hardware. These are optional
and cost/hardware-bearing; making them mandatory would break the core promise.

## Naming risk (linked to DEC-1)

The market contains ShikkhaPlus, Shikkha, ShikshaSuite, Sikhsha, ShikshaCoach,
Shikkha IT, and CoachDesk. **"MERQO ShikkhaDesk"** must be used as the full brand
consistently, and a formal trademark review is recommended before paid
distribution. **No silent rename.**
